# Matheus Costa Monteiro

<h3>
  AI Research Engineer | LLMs &amp; Industrial AI | MLOps | M.Sc. Student at USP | C4AI
</h3>

I am an AI Research Engineer working across large language models, industrial AI, and machine learning systems. My focus includes post-training, data engineering, rigorous model evaluation, and reproducible workflows that connect research with practical engineering.

I am an M.Sc. student at the University of São Paulo (USP) and work with C4AI on Brazilian Portuguese language models. In projects such as LLMBR, I contribute to post-training and data engineering, including dataset curation, tokenization, evaluation workflows, data preparation for pretraining and instruction tuning, and tooling for LLM development.

In parallel, I contribute to research and engineering in the TRACTIAN–USP collaboration, focusing on industrial condition monitoring with servomotors as an application case. This work connects signal analysis, feature engineering, and evaluation across operating conditions with the development of MLOps tools for engineers.

## Background

My experience spans software engineering, data engineering, machine learning engineering, and applied research. Today, my work brings together LLM post-training, industrial condition monitoring, and the tools needed to make ML experiments reproducible and useful to engineers.

My undergraduate research at the Federal University of Uberlândia (UFU) focused on scheduling algorithms for heterogeneous GPU environments. Together with competitive programming and backend development, it shaped my approach to algorithms, performance, and experimental evaluation.
